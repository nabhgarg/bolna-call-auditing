# Deep Research Briefs (20)

Chosen to stress citation reliability in different ways: contested topics (easy to
over-claim), fast-moving topics (stale sources), numeric topics (precise figures
must match sources), niche topics (thin sourcing → fabrication risk), and two
control topics (well-documented, should score high — gives the audit a baseline
and keeps it fair).

Run each verbatim as the Deep Research query.

## Numeric / data-heavy (figures must match the source exactly)
1. "What is the current market size of the AI browser-assistant / AI copilot consumer market, and what are the main growth forecasts through 2030? Include specific figures with sources."
2. "Compare the subscription prices and free-tier limits of ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro, Perplexity Pro, and Poe as of today. Cite each."
3. "How much funding have AI model-routing startups (Martian, NotDiamond, OpenRouter, Unify) raised, from whom, and at what valuations where known?"
4. "What percentage of enterprise AI projects fail to reach production, according to published studies from 2023–2026? List each study's figure and methodology."
5. "What are the published token prices of the major LLM APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) right now, and how have they changed over the past 18 months?"

## Fast-moving (stale-source trap)
6. "What are the most recent major AI model releases from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google, and what benchmarks did each publish at launch?"
7. "What is the current status of the EU AI Act's implementation — which obligations are in force today and which take effect next?"
8. "Summarize the latest research (past 12 months) on LLM routing and model-selection systems, citing specific papers."

## Contested / over-claim trap
9. "Does using AI assistants reduce critical thinking skills? Review the actual published evidence on both sides."
10. "Is AI-generated code more or less secure than human-written code? What do published studies actually measure and find?"
11. "What does research say about whether chain-of-thought prompting genuinely improves LLM reasoning versus just improving output formatting?"
12. "What is the evidence that AI companions/chatbots affect loneliness, positively or negatively? Cite studies, sample sizes, and limitations."

## Niche / thin-sourcing (fabrication risk)
13. "What is the history and current state of human-in-the-loop evaluation marketplaces for AI (Scale, Surge, Mercor, Handshake AI)? Include founding dates, headcounts, and known clients with sources."
14. "What public information exists about how Perplexity's model fallback/downgrade system works, and what controversies has it caused?"
15. "What happened during the GPT-5 launch router controversy in August 2025 — timeline, what broke, and how OpenAI responded? Cite primary sources."
16. "What academic work exists on evaluating AI browser extensions and wrapper products specifically (not the underlying models)?"

## India-angle (tests non-US sourcing)
17. "What is the size and growth of India's consumer AI-subscription market, and which Indian AI consumer startups have raised institutional funding since 2024?"
18. "How are Indian IT-services companies (TCS, Infosys, Wipro) deploying LLMs internally, per their own filings, earnings calls, and credible reporting?"

## Controls (well-documented; should score high)
19. "Explain the transformer architecture's history: the key papers from 2017 onward, their authors, and each paper's main contribution."
20. "What were the findings of the Stanford/MIT study on generative AI and customer-support-agent productivity (Brynjolfsson et al.)? Sample, method, effect sizes."
